Relation to Traffic OS

This project extends the team's earlier project (Traffic OS: Real-Time Traffic Light Scheduling System) by solving the predictive question: instead of only reacting to queues at the current second, Spark predicts where congestion will form 15 minutes ahead across 12 junctions to allow adaptive pre-emption.
